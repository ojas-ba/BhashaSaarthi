Successful observed safeguards: `Naman ne 12 November ko report bheji thi` produced `Naman ने 12 November को report भेजी थी`; the email example retained `test@example.com` exactly. `kal` alone remained `kal`, with candidates/warnings available rather than a forced past/future interpretation.

The basic evaluation completed 35/50 requested actions, including one model-free passthrough; 34 generated cases were quality-scored. Reference CER 0.09123, WER 0.21303 and exact match 0.44118 are conditional on those completed generated cases, not all 50 inputs. Excluding failed cases can overstate quality if reported without coverage. No completed translation score, human adequacy/fluency score or Param1 ablation is available.

Raw IndicXlit diagnostics are recorded per case and aggregated separately. Comparing raw Hindi-script output against Natural Hinglish references penalizes its English-word transliterations by design; it is a component diagnostic, not an Aksharantar word-level benchmark. Context-aware versus phrasing comparisons remain unmeasured because Param1 could not run. No model prompts were tuned on these held-out outputs.
